AI Signal

A quiet place
for your newsletters.

AI Signal is a personal, Kindle-inspired reader for emails in the Gmail label _AI_SIGNAL.

Read the newest editions in one feed, filter by newsletter, and choose your text size and reading layout. The reader works in your iPhone browser and can be added to the Home Screen.

Your connection

The app requests read-only Gmail access to display labelled newsletters. The private hosted reader saves an encrypted Google connection on its server so you can return without signing in to Gmail each time. It does not send, change or delete email.

Open private reader

The reader requires its owner's Sites sign-in. This public page contains app information only; it has no access to your Gmail.
